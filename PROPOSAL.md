# Personal Site Proposal

## 1. Overview
This website is a personal digital presence for me: a place where people can quickly understand who I am, what I care about, what I have built, and the life behind the work. It should feel personal, honest, and grounded in story rather than just a résumé page.

The site is designed for two main audiences:
- Recruiters who want to understand my background, skills, and trajectory
- Friends and acquaintances who want to know me more deeply beyond the surface

The central idea is simple: the website should help people understand my journey, my projects, my hobbies, and the life context that shaped me.

## 2. Core audience
### Recruiters
A recruiter or hiring manager lands here to answer a few questions quickly:
- Who is this person?
- What kind of work have they done?
- What do they care about?
- What makes them interesting, thoughtful, and valuable?

They are looking for signal, not noise. They want a clean, digestible overview of my skills, work, and personal perspective.

### Friends and personal connections
Friends may visit the site to see a more human side of me:
- What I have built
- What I am passionate about
- My hobbies and life outside work
- The story behind the person they know

This is not just a portfolio. It is a life snapshot and a story.

## 3. Primary user action
The single most important thing a visitor should do is this:

Understand who I am as a person and as a builder.

That means they should be able to see:
- my journey and growth
- the things I have built
- my hobbies and interests
- the context of my life
- the personality behind the work

The site should answer: "What kind of person is this, and why should I care?"

## 4. Site purpose
This personal site should function as a living identity page:
- a professional profile
- a creative portfolio
- a personal story
- a gallery of life and interests

It should not feel like a sterile company website. It should feel like a digital version of me.

## 5. Recommended sections
### Home
The homepage should be the first impression. It should introduce who I am in a compact, warm, compelling way.

Suggested content:
- short headline and intro
- one sentence on who I am
- a summary of my journey and interests
- a callout to projects, gallery, and about section
- a few visual moments: photo, work snapshot, favorite hobby image

The homepage should answer: "Who is this person, and what is this site about?"

### About
This is where the story lives.

Suggested content:
- short bio
- background/context
- values or beliefs
- life story and turning points
- what motivates me
- what I care about beyond work

This section should feel personal and honest, not overly polished or corporate.

### Projects
This is the portfolio section for the things I have built.

Suggested content:
- project names
- short descriptions
- the problem or goal behind each project
- technologies or tools used
- outcome or learnings
- links to live demos, repositories, or case studies

This section is especially important for recruiters, but it should still feel like a reflection of my thinking and process.

### Gallery
This should show the personal side of me.

Suggested content:
- photos from life and travel
- hobbies and moments from everyday life
- creative snapshots
- visual memories that shape who I am

The gallery should feel human, intimate, and layered with context.

## 6. Content available now
At this stage, the site can rely on a mix of personal content and story fragments. The most important thing is to keep it authentic.

Current content likely includes:
- photos
- biography text
- context about who I am and where I came from
- a collection of life moments
- projects and things I built
- hobbies and personal interests

This is enough to build a strong personal site without needing a highly formal brand presentation.

## 7. Tone and aesthetic direction
The site should feel:
- personal
- thoughtful
- modern
- warm and human
- creative but not chaotic

It should not feel like a generic portfolio template. It should feel like a place where someone can get a genuine sense of my personality.

## 8. Success criteria
The site is successful if a visitor can do all of the following in a few minutes:
- understand who I am
- understand what I have built
- understand what I enjoy
- understand how my life and work connect
- feel like they know me a little better after visiting

## 9. Recommended structure
A good practical structure for the site could be:

1. Home
2. About
3. Projects
4. Gallery
5. Contact or links

Optional extras:
- resume download
- social links
- favorite books / music / inspiration
- writing or notes section

## 10. Final direction
This site should not only present my work. It should present my life in context.

It should show that I am not just a collection of skills or accomplishments. I am a person with a journey, a point of view, a creative life, and projects that reflect who I am.

The website should help someone leave with a clear answer to this question:

"I understand who this person is, what they care about, and what kind of life and work they are building."

That is the goal.
